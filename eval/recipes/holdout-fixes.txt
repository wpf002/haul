Held-Out Fixes
Serves 4

Ingredients
- 1 Tbsp. + 2 tsp. olive oil (divided)
- 1 tsp tumeric powder
- 1.5 lb / 750 g chicken thigh fillets, cut into bite size pieces
- 4 carrots cut into 2-inch chunks
- Pinch salt
- ½ tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- Flour or corn tortillas
- 3 cups neutral oil ((for deep-frying; enough for 1½ inches (3.8 cm) of oil in the pot))
- 2 tsp smoked or sweet paprika
- 1 cup chopped fresh cilantro leaves and stems (patted dry)
- 1 and 1/3 cups (320ml) warm water (about 110°F/43°C)
- 1 (5 to 6 pound) roasting chicken
- 1 bulb of fennel, tops removed, and cut into wedges
- 15 oz canned or frozen corn (drained)
- 1 cup long- or short-grain brown rice* (rinsed)
- 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice (plus wedges for serving)
- 1 Tbsp green onion or parsley (to garnish)
- 3 and 1/2 to 4 cups (438–500g) unbleached all-purpose flour (spooned and leveled)
- 1 cup fresh parsley leaves
