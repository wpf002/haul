Sheet Pan Chicken Fajitas
Serves 4

Ingredients
- 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 2 red bell peppers, sliced
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 8 flour tortillas
- 2 limes
- 1 avocado
- Sour cream, for serving
